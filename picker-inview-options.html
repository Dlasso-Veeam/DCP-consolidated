Picker — marking the day in view

As the restore-point list scrolls, the calendar marks the day at the top of it. The solid green day is always the restore point you have chosen and never moves with scroll; the options below only differ in how the in-view day reads. Scroll the list, click a date, click a time. The tinted day header in the list is the same in every option.
